# CHAVÉR — Shopify Setup

This repository contains the CHAVÉR Foundations Shopify theme and pre-launch storefront structure.

## Required Shopify Admin setup

### Pages
Create these pages in Shopify Admin > Online Store > Pages and assign the matching theme template:

- Founder Story → `page.founder-story`
- Contact → `page.contact`
- FAQ → `page.faq`
- Size Guide → `page.size-guide`
- Customer Care → `page.customer-care`

Recommended handles:

- `/pages/founder-story`
- `/pages/contact`
- `/pages/faq`
- `/pages/size-guide`
- `/pages/customer-care`

### Store policies
In Shopify Admin > Settings > Policies, complete and publish:

- Refund / Returns policy
- Privacy policy
- Terms of service
- Shipping policy

The footer already links to Shopify's standard policy URLs.

Before publishing policies, replace all placeholders and verify business name, contact details, return address, shipping countries, delivery estimates, refund windows and applicable consumer-law requirements.

### Products
Create the FOUNDATIONS COLLECTION products in Shopify and add:

- Product title
- Product description
- Final price
- Product photography
- Variants / colours / sizes
- Inventory when ordering opens

Current theme is intentionally configured as PRE-LAUNCH. Checkout messaging indicates ordering opens Q1 2027.

### Collection
Create or confirm a collection containing the CHAVÉR Foundations products. The theme uses Shopify's all-products collection as a safe default.

### Newsletter
The theme uses Shopify customer/newsletter forms. Confirm Shopify Email / your preferred email marketing integration is active before public launch.

### Navigation
The theme includes built-in header and footer links. Keep page handles consistent with the list above.

### Domain and launch
Before publishing:

1. Connect the final CHAVÉR domain.
2. Confirm sender email authentication.
3. Test desktop and mobile storefront.
4. Test contact form and newsletter submission.
5. Verify policy text and legal/business details.
6. Confirm checkout remains disabled until CHAVÉR is ready to accept orders.
7. When ordering opens, replace the pre-launch cart/product messaging and enable normal Shopify purchasing.
